## Method notes

* Both methods call the deployed `predict_proba`, so they explain the model actually used.
* Occlusion changes ONE feature at a time and can miss interactions; permutation importance can be distorted by correlated features. SHAP (optional) has its own caveats.
* No demographic attribute is ever modified to build a 'lower-risk scenario'.
